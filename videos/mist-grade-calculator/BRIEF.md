# BRIEF — MIST Grade Calculator launch film

## The one thing
You can now find out exactly how many marks you need in the final exam to reach your
target grade — before the final, not after it.

## The one thing to remember
"One number, and you know if it's still possible."

## One viewer, one real task
A MIST undergraduate who has three class tests, a mid term, attendance and performance
already in hand, and one exam left. They type their marks into the sheet, and the sheet
tells them the number the Final has to be.

One real piece of data travels through the film: the target grade A+ = 240 marks on a
300-mark semester, and the answer that comes back.

## Length
30 s (a range of 26–34 s is acceptable; the story sets the exact cut)

## Aspect
1920 x 1080, landscape, 30 fps

## Voice
No voiceover. The product's own text and large titles carry the words, over music.
The site's copy is used verbatim.

## Music
One music bed, generated locally with Python (MusicGen). Mood: focused, quietly
propulsive, editorial — a paper-and-ink ledger with a pulse under it. No lyrics.

## Real source of truth
https://mistgc.vercel.app/ — captured. The captured screenshots are the film's product.
Nothing that claims to be the product is a mockup.

## Worlds
1. The sheet on paper (cool concrete paper, ink rules, volt accent)
2. The ink band (the verdict panel, dark navy, the number in condensed caps)
3. The campus at dusk (the site's duotone photographs)

## What is absent
No voiceover, no fabricated UI, no invented numbers, no avatars, no stock footage.

## Facts that may appear on screen (all from the captured site)
- 300 marks in a 3-credit semester, 180 of them the Final
- 200 marks in a 2-credit semester, 120 of them the Final
- A+ = 240 marks = 4.00 CGPA; the grade ladder runs A+ to F*
- CTs: 3 x 20, best two count, weighted x 1.5 outside GEBS-101
- Mid term 30, attendance 15, performance 15
- The site's own lines: "Your final, worked out" / "Enter your marks. The sheet says what
  the Final must be." / "Six marks on the sheet" / "The Final is what is left." /
  "Two credits, or three" / "The course you pick sets the weightings." /
  "What each grade takes" / "MIST at the hour the semester ends"
